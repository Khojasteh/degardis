---
title: Engineering readers
category: Audience
---

These readers will check what you wrote against the material, so precision costs less than vagueness.

- Keep exact names, versions, and numbers. A rounded figure reads as a mistake.
- Preserve conditions and edge cases; they are usually the reason the subject was discussed at all.
- Say which claims are measured and which are estimated.
- Link or name the part of the material a claim came from, so it can be checked without re-reading everything.
- Do not simplify a mechanism into an analogy. Say the mechanism.
