---
title: Research papers
category: Material
description: Formal writing whose claims are already qualified, and whose qualifiers are the first thing summaries lose.
---

A paper states its claims carefully and its limitations in one place near the end. A summary that keeps the first and drops the second overstates everything.

- Carry the limitations section. It qualifies every result above it.
- Keep the population, sample size, and conditions attached to any figure you quote.
- Distinguish what was measured from what the authors conclude from it. Those are different claims with different support.
- Where the abstract and the results disagree in emphasis, the results decide.
- Related work is context, not findings. It rarely belongs in a summary at all.
