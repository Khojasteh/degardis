---
title: Meeting transcripts
category: Material
description: Spoken material where positions are revised as the discussion goes on.
guides:
- transcript-passes
---

A transcript records how a group got somewhere, not where it ended up. Read it
through before writing anything.

- Track each subject's position over time and carry only the last one, unless the
  history explains it.
- Speech repeats. Three restatements of one point are one claim.
- Attribute by speaker, since a transcript is the one material where who said
  something is always recorded.
- Watch for decisions made in passing. The sentence that settles a subject is
  often shorter than the ten that argued about it.
- Where the transcript is automatic, treat names, numbers, and technical terms as
  unreliable, and say so rather than silently correcting them.
