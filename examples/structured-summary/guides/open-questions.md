---
title: Recording an open question
applicability:
- When the result records a question the material does not settle
---

An open question is a finding, not a gap in the result. Write each one so the reader can act on it without the material:

- State the question as the reader would ask it, not as the material phrased it.
- Give each position the material takes on it, attributed to whoever holds it.
- Name what would settle it: a document, a decision, a measurement, or a person.

Do not choose between positions the material leaves open, and do not drop one because it was stated less often.
