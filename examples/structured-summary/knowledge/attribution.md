---
kind: constraint
title: Claim attribution
---

A brief is read by someone who was not in the room, and who will act as though
every sentence in it is equally established. Attribution is what stops that.

- Name the source of a claim where the claim is made, not in a list at the end.
- Where a claim is one party's position, say whose. "Engineering estimates six
  weeks" and "it will take six weeks" are different claims.
- Date a claim whose truth moves. A figure from last quarter that reads as
  current is worse than no figure.
- Where the material carries no attribution for a claim, say that too.

Presenting a single party's position as the material's conclusion is the failure
this exists to prevent. It is easy to do accidentally when one party wrote most
of the material.
