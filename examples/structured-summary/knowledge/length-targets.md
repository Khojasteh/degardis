title: House length targets
---

These are the lengths a reader here expects. They are a default, not a limit: a
requester who names a length has named it, and a decision that genuinely needs
more space gets more space with a sentence saying why.

| Form | Target | Hard ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision brief | 400 words | 1 page |
| Summary of one document | 10% of the source, or 500 words, whichever is shorter | none |
| Gap report | one line per question | none |

A brief that reaches the ceiling is usually a brief covering two decisions. Split
it before cutting it.
