---
kind: constraint
title: Source fidelity
---

The material is the only authority. Nothing you know from elsewhere may enter the
result as though the material said it.

- Do not resolve a question the material leaves open. Report it as open.
- Do not raise a hedge into a statement. "The team thought it was likely" does
  not become "it is likely".
- Do not merge two sources into one claim. If they differ, say both.
- Do not fill an obvious gap from general knowledge, however certain you are.
  Name the gap instead.

Where the requester has asked something the material does not answer, say so and
say what would answer it. That is a useful result. A confident answer assembled
from outside the material is not, and the requester has no way to tell the two
apart.
