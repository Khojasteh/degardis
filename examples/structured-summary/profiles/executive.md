---
title: Executive readers
category: Audience
description: Readers who will act on the first two sentences and may not read the rest.
---

Write so that stopping early still leaves the reader correct, rather than
uninformed.

- Lead with the decision or the state of things, not with how it was reached.
- Put money, dates, and risk in the first paragraph if they are in the brief at
  all.
- One page. A second page is read by nobody who was not already going to ask.
- Name what you need from them explicitly: a decision, an approval, or nothing.
- Keep the qualifier in the same sentence as the claim. A caveat in a later
  paragraph is a caveat that was not read.
