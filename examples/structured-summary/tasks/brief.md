---
title: Write a decision brief
recognize:
- the requester needs to decide something and wants the material reduced to what bears on that decision
- the requester names an audience and asks what to tell them
- the requester asks for a brief, a memo, or a one-pager for someone else to read
goal: >-
  A named reader can make the decision in front of them from the brief alone,
  and can see what the decision rests on.
knowledge:
- source-fidelity
- attribution
- audience
- choose-detail
- length-targets
guides:
- summary-template
---

A brief is organized around the decision, not around the material. Two documents
that bear on the same choice belong in the same paragraph of the brief even if
they were written years apart, and a long document that bears on nothing the
reader has to decide contributes a sentence at most.

Settle who the reader is before writing. If the requester has not said, and the
material does not make it plain, ask — the answer changes the whole brief, so no
default is safe here.
