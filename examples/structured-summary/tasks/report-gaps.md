---
title: Report what the material does not settle
recognize:
- the requester asks whether the material answers a question
- the requester asks what is missing, unresolved, or contradictory in what they supplied
- a summary or brief cannot be written because the material does not cover the subject asked about
goal: >-
  The requester knows which of their questions the material settles, which it
  leaves open, and where two parts of it disagree.
knowledge:
- source-fidelity
- source-material
- structure-subjects
---

Establish the set of questions first, from what the requester asked and from what
the material claims to cover. Then account for every one of them: settled, open,
or contradicted. A question you do not mention reads as a question the material
answered.

Report a contradiction as a contradiction, giving both sides and where each came
from. Choosing between them is the requester's to do, and doing it for them
silently destroys the only evidence they had that there was a choice.

Run [[script:list_headings.py]] when the material is long enough that its own
headings are the fastest way to enumerate its subjects.
