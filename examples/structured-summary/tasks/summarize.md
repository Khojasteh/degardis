---
title: Summarize supplied material
recognize:
- the requester hands over material and asks what it says
- the requester asks for a summary, a digest, or a shorter version of something
- the requester asks to be caught up on a document, thread, or transcript
goal: >-
  The requester has a shorter text that carries every subject the material settles, at a depth they can act on, and asserts nothing the material does not.
knowledge:
- source-fidelity
- source-material
- structure-subjects
- choose-detail
guides:
- summary-template
---

Read the whole of the material before writing any of the summary. A summary written while still reading takes the order of the source as the order of the result, and the source was not written to be read in one pass.
